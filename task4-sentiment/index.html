Method: labeled datasets use a negation-aware Naive Bayes bag-of-words classifier with stratified 5-fold out-of-fold validation; low-confidence predictions are called neutral. Unlabelled datasets use the transparent polarity lexicon and neutral threshold. Emotion signals use word lists for joy, trust, anger, sadness, fear, disgust, anticipation, and surprise.

Predictions use stratified 5-fold out-of-fold Naive Bayes classification with a negation-aware bag of words; neutral is reserved for low-confidence scores. The supplied labels contain positive/negative examples only, so neutral predictions cannot be validated against a neutral ground-truth class.

Lexicon matches can miss sarcasm, context, domain-specific meanings, and mixed opinions. Use these counts as a screening summary, not as a substitute for human review or a full contextual language model.
